> 规则：每条 ≤500 字符，开头用【标签】概括，讲清"解决什么问题"。

**【1｜Single Pack, No Extra Cost】**
Most grab handle belts are sold in pairs. This one comes as a single belt, so you only pay for what you actually use — ideal if you usually ride with one passenger, or if you want to try the setup before committing to a pair. Fits riders of most body types through the adjustable woven strap.

**【2｜Two Textured Grips Right at the Waist】**
Unlike asking a passenger to hold onto your jacket or a backpack strap, this belt gives them two dedicated handholds with molded, grooved surfaces that stay easy to find by touch. Each side sits within natural reach, so your passenger keeps balance without pulling on you.

**【3｜Wide Mesh Padding Spreads the Load】**
The back panel uses a wide, breathable mesh pad instead of a narrow strap. That spreads the pulling force across a larger area, which keeps the belt comfortable on longer rides and reduces pressure points around the waist. Padding is stitched into a reinforced fabric shell.

**【4｜No Drilling, No Brackets, No Permanent Changes】**
The belt wraps around the rider's waist and fastens with a quick-release buckle — nothing is bolted to the bike. Put it on before you set off, take it off when you stop, and store it with your riding gear. Works on touring bikes, cruisers and most motorcycles set up for a passenger.

**【5｜Built to Take Load: Steel Rivets and Double Stitching】**
Reinforced attachment points use steel rivets, double-stitched nylon webbing and bound fabric edges to hold the handles securely. Note: this is a **handhold aid for a passenger, not a safety restraint or seat belt** — always check strap tension, buckle engagement and your passenger's reach before riding.
